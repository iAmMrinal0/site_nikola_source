I used to dabble with Python, writing scripts, cli and web apps to solve trivial tasks. I have tried my hand at desktop application development, Windows 10/Windows Phone app development, however I am drawn to the Web. To check out my projects, head over to [GitHub](https://github.com/iammrinal0).

I did some work with Haskell/PureScript while I was at [Juspay](https://juspay.in).

Currently, I am playing with payment providers at [Boozt](https://boozt.com) in Malmö, Sweden, using Haskell, Dhall, Nix, SQL.

When I am not wasting time, I solve [Sudoku](http://websudoku.com/), listen to [music](https://last.fm/user/iammrinal0), go out on a [run](https://strava.com/athletes/iammrinal0) or tinker with my home server setup or play some Rocket League.

Want to collaborate on a project or get in touch with me? The best way is Twitter or mail.
